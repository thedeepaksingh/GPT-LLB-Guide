## Semester 1 paper map — verify cohort-specific scheme
The CCSU-hosted amended three-year LL.B. syllabus (w.e.f. 2017–18) lists five 100-mark papers:
1. Jurisprudence I (Legal Theory) — K-1001
2. Constitutional Law of India I (Nature of the Constitution and Fundamental Rights) — K-1002
3. Law of Torts — K-1003
4. Law of Crimes I (Indian Penal Code) — K-1004 in the legacy scheme
5. Contract I (General Principles of Contract) — K-1005

**Major change to criminal-law paper:** CCSU’s January 2026 examination programme identifies *Law of Crimes I — Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)*, code K-1006, as starting w.e.f. 2024–25, while legacy IPC code K-1004 remains for ex/back-paper students. Confirm the scheme applicable to your admission session and exam category with IEC Law College before studying the criminal paper. See [official source register](sources/ccsu-syllabus.md). Do not blend IPC and BNS section numbers.

## Study method
Every paper will be developed separately. Each topic should include: exact syllabus mapping; core explanation; definitions and competing theories; historical context; applicable legislation and current version where relevant; verified cases with facts, issue, holding and ratio; critiques and counter-arguments; examples/problem application; short, medium and long model answers; likely questions; case/statute tables; flashcards and revision tests; sources and verification date.

## Accuracy rules
- Official syllabus, legislation, Gazette notifications, and court judgments take priority.
- A previous-paper pattern is labeled by its paper/year/source and is never presented as a guarantee.
- Distinguish ratio decidendi from obiter, majority from dissent, binding from persuasive authority.
- Record legal change with enactment, commencement, applicability and saving/transitional provisions.
- Verify case names, citations and legal propositions before relying on them.
- Learn how to apply a principle; a list of case names alone is not a complete answer.

## Versioning
- Material is timestamped in Asia/Kolkata (IST, UTC+05:30).
- Substantive legal updates carry a “law checked as of” date and official-source link.
- Large changes should be placed on a topic branch and logged in `CHANGELOG.md`.
