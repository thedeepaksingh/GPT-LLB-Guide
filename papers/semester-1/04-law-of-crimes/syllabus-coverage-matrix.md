**Important:** This matrix is a study map, not a substitute for the Act. Verify the exact subsection, exception, punishment, amendment and commencement status from the official consolidated BNS text before writing a section number in an exam. The syllabus is topic-led and does not itself prescribe that every row will be asked.

## Statutory verification method

For every offence, prepare a six-column mini-table:

1. Provision and exact wording
2. Ingredients (number each element)
3. Mental element and causation
4. Exceptions/defences
5. Punishment and any special rule
6. One case authority and a short application

Primary source: [India Code — Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a202345.pdf).
