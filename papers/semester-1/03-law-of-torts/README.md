## Coverage
- Definition, nature, development and constituents of tort
- *Damnum sine injuria* and *injuria sine damno*
- Malice, motive, capacity, joint tortfeasors and general defences
- Vicarious liability and employer responsibility
- State liability and sovereign immunity
- Strict liability and absolute liability
- Negligence, *res ipsa loquitur*, causation and remoteness
- Nuisance, trespass, assault, battery, false imprisonment and defamation
- Malicious prosecution, thin-skull rule and remedies
- Consumer Protection Act, 2019, including consumer/service concepts and redressal overview
- Short answers, essay answers, comparisons and worked hypotheticals
