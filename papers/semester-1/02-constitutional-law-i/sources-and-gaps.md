## 3. Current-law rules
- Use the latest official constitutional text and note amendment dates where relevant.
- A case citation is not a substitute for its holding. Check majority/dissent, ratio, later treatment and subsequent constitutional amendments.
- Article 19 grounds of restriction differ by clause; always quote the relevant clause.
- Article 12 status is fact-sensitive; no one factor is universally decisive.
- Article 21 rights and tests develop over time; verify the latest binding authority for the specific issue.
- Article 22 is constitutional text. For arrest procedure, separately identify the procedural statute applicable to the date/facts; BNSS replaced CrPC for relevant matters from 1 July 2024, subject to savings/transitional provisions.
- Constitutional amendment review under Article 368/basic structure is not identical to ordinary law review under Article 13.
- The Preamble’s wording was amended by the Constitution (42nd Amendment) Act, 1976; check official text and amendment source before quoting historical detail.
