## Primary university sources

### 1. CCSU amended three-year LL.B. syllabus
- **Title:** *LL.B. 3 Year (Six Semester) Course — Amended Syllabus (w.e.f. 2017–18 Session)*
- **Official PDF:** https://cdn.ccsuniversity.ac.in/public/pdf/2024/04/syl%28LL.B.%203%20Year%20%28Six%20Semester%29%20Course%29.pdf
- **What it establishes:** Semester I has K-1001 Jurisprudence I; K-1002 Constitutional Law I; K-1003 Law of Torts; K-1004 Law of Crimes I (IPC); K-1005 Contract I. Each is listed for 100 marks in the scheme.
- **K-1001 outline (syllabus text):** Introduction—definition, nature, scope, importance; Natural Law School—classical natural law and revival (Rudolf Stammler and Kohler); Analytical School—John Austin, Hans Kelsen, H.L.A. Hart; Historical School—Savigny and Sir Henry Sumner Maine; Sociological School—background/characteristics, Roscoe Pound and Leon Duguit; American Modern Realism; Economic Approach of Law—Marxist economic approach.
- **Recommended authors listed by CCSU:** Bodenheimer, Dias, Friedman, Lloyd, V.D. Mahajan, Paton and Salmond.
- **Caution:** This is an amended syllabus explicitly effective from 2017–18, so do not assume it settles every later batch’s version. Check the latest IEC notice and your cohort.

## Authorities used as illustrations in jurisprudence
- *Kesavananda Bharati v State of Kerala*, (1973) 4 SCC 225 — limits on constitutional amendment / basic structure.
- *Maneka Gandhi v Union of India*, (1978) 1 SCC 248 — fair, just and reasonable procedure in Article 21 interpretation.
- *Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v Union of India*, (2017) 10 SCC 1 — privacy and constitutional values.
- *Navtej Singh Johar v Union of India*, (2018) 10 SCC 1 — dignity, autonomy and constitutional morality.
- *R v Dudley and Stephens* (1884) 14 QBD 273 — necessity, morality and legal responsibility in a classic common-law illustration.

**Note:** These cases are examples that illuminate theories of law and morality; they do not replace philosopher/doctrine coverage in K-1001. Case-specific paragraphs and later treatment should be verified against primary judgments before final exam use.
