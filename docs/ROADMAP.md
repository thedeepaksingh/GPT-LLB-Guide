## 3. What “complete topic coverage” means
For each examinable subtopic, record: exact syllabus anchor; definition and core concept; historical development; current legislation; verified authorities with facts/issue/holding/ratio; critiques; examples and hypotheticals; short/medium/long model answers; revision aids; source URL and verification date; and any uncertainty flags.

## 4. Common omissions to prevent
- Confusing three-year LL.B. with B.A. LL.B. or another university's scheme.
- Learning IPC when the cohort is examined on BNS, or vice versa.
- Using outdated statutory sections or consumer pecuniary thresholds.
- Writing case names without ratio, application or subsequent treatment.
- Treating coaching-site “important questions” as university-confirmed.
- Omitting defences, causation, remedies, counterarguments and problem application.
- Confusing ratio, obiter, majority and dissent.
- Treating a past exam structure as guaranteed for the next sitting.
- Relying on AI summaries without checking primary law.
